v1.0.0 Latest
Aug 12, 2026

The first public release — the bridge between your Telegram bots and any LLM, local or cloud.

  • Menu bar app — start and stop your bots, open settings and quit, all from the menu bar.
  • Multiple bots — one per token, each with its own model, personality and skills.
  • Bring your own brain — mlx-serve, Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter — with automatic fallbacks when a provider goes down.
  • 30+ model-driven tools — reminders, weather, stocks, crypto, polls, dice, expenses, habits, bookmarks, RSS feeds, translation, currency, PDF & receipt scanning, memory, digests and briefings.
  • Keyless data sources — weather, currency, Wikipedia, SEC filings — no API keys required.
  • Telegram-native — streaming replies, typing dots, animated dice, native polls, voice notes in and out.
  • Local-first privacy — chat history, memories and skills live in ~/.mlx-serve on your Mac.
  • macOS 13+, Apple Silicon. Unsigned build — right-click → Open on first launch.
